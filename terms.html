Accounts

  • You do not need one. Catching, reviewing and keeping sets, and exporting them, work without an account. An account is what backs your friends, your @username and your share links; you make one by tapping Sign in.
  • One person, one account. Your @username and display name are yours to choose, and they may not be someone else's name, a brand you do not own, or a word meant to mislead. We can change or remove a handle that breaks this.
  • You can leave at any time. Signing out keeps your sets on your phone and nothing else. Profile → Settings → Privacy & FAQ → Delete my data from Catchy's server removes the account and everything the server held for it, the way the privacy page describes.

Your sets and your words

A set you catch is yours: the songs, the times, the clips you chose to keep, the names you proposed, the stars, the note, the card and the link. Sharing a set with friends, or making a link for it, gives Catchy only what it needs to show that set to the people you shared it with, in the app and on the shared page, until you unshare it. Nothing you share is sold or used for ads, and it is shown only to your friends and to anyone holding a link you made. A reaction on a shared set carries the @username of whoever reacted, as the privacy page says. A friend's copy of your set stays theirs until you unshare it; then the shared page and the copies go.

The rules

Shared sets carry other people's words: a proposed song name, a comment, a reaction, a name on a card. So a few rules apply to what you share or post:

  • Do not pretend to be someone else, and do not post anyone's private details — an address, a phone number, a photo of them without their say.
  • Do not harass, threaten or demean anyone, in a comment or in a name.
  • Do not post anything that is illegal where you are, or that you do not have the right to share.
  • Do not use the app, its pages or its server by machine — scraping, automated accounts, or anything that reaches past what the app shows you.

Objectionable content and abusive people are not tolerated here. Something that breaks these rules comes down, and the account that posted it can be closed, on the first time if it is bad enough. We tell you when we do, unless the law says we cannot.

Reporting something

If a comment, a proposed name, a shared set or a profile breaks the rules, report it by email to support@joincatchy.com; in the app, Profile → Settings → Privacy & FAQ → Report a name sends one about a name. We read every report and act on the ones that break the rules within 24 hours: the content comes down, and a person who posted it can lose their account. We tell you what we did. If you are the one reported, the same mail reaches us.

Catchy Pro

Catchy Pro is a subscription sold through the App Store and billed to your Apple ID: a monthly or a yearly plan, with a free trial where the store shows one. The price shown in the app is the price. It renews on its own until you cancel it in your iPhone's Settings under Subscriptions; cancelling stops the next charge and keeps Pro until the period you paid for ends. Refunds are Apple's to give, through the App Store. Capturing a set, reviewing it and sharing it stay free. Always.

What Catchy is not

  • Not a guarantee. Catchy names songs by matching what it hears against a catalog. A loud room, a remix or a song it has never heard gives a wrong name, a "possible match" or an Unknown moment. Check before you rely on it.
  • Not a recording. The sound itself never leaves your phone. Audio is kept only where the app will ask you to judge it, and while a match waits for a connection, as the privacy page says; a set is a list, not a tape.
  • Not always on. The app can stop listening when iOS takes the microphone or the battery runs low; it tells you, and it does not promise every song.
